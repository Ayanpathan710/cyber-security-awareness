Phishing: The Fake Message Trap

Attackers create urgency so people act before they verify.

URGENT: ACCOUNT ACTION REQUIRED

Your account will be locked today. Verify your information immediately to avoid losing access.

VERIFY NOW

Never rush a security decision.

RED FLAGS TO WATCH FOR

  • Urgency or fear — "act now or lose access"
  • Unknown or lookalike sender address
  • Suspicious or shortened links
  • Request for password, OTP, or card details
  • Spelling and grammar mistakes
  • Offers that feel too good to be true
06

Your First Line of Defense

Strong, unique passwords + MFA make account takeover harder.

PASSWORD

Use a long, unique password or passphrase.
  • Never reuse the same password everywhere.
  • Aim for 12+ characters, mixing words & symbols.
  • Use a trusted password manager.

MFA

Something you KNOW + Something you HAVE

Password + Authenticator / OTP

  • Turn on MFA for email, banking & social media.
  • Prefer an authenticator app over SMS where possible.
07

Social Engineering

Sometimes the easiest target is not the computer — it is the person.

FEAR

"Your account is blocked."

URGENCY

"Act within 10 minutes."

TRUST

"I am from support."

REWARD

"You won a prize."

DEFENSE: Pause  •  Verify independently  •  Never share OTPs or passwords

08

Ransomware

Malware can encrypt files and disrupt access to systems.

YOUR FILES ARE LOCKED

FILE_1.docx

FILE_2.xlsx

FILE_3.pdf

FILE_4.jpg

IMPACT: Data unavailable • Business disruption • Recovery costs

PREVENTION

  • Keep software and systems updated.
  • Back up important files regularly — ideally offline.
  • Only download from trusted, official sources.
  • Don't open unexpected attachments or macros.
  • Stay alert: awareness stops most ransomware at the door.
09

Public Wi-Fi: Convenience vs. Caution

Treat unfamiliar networks as untrusted.

PUBLIC Wi-Fi

UNKNOWN NETWORK

  • Avoid sensitive transactions (banking, shopping) when possible
  • Verify the exact network name before connecting
  • Use HTTPS sites — check for the padlock
  • Turn off auto-connect to open networks
  • Use mobile data or a VPN for anything important
  • Disconnect and forget the network when finished
